Shield, Turn & Go
0/5
  • Equipment: 6-7 balls, ~9 cones (3 squares + 3 escape cones), no props.
  • Objective: one teaching step past Monday — from "hold my ground" to "feel the push, turn away, dribble to the escape cone".
  • Setup: three 10x10y squares; in each square a 1v1 — a defender (no ball) pressures the carrier from behind; an escape cone sits ~5-10 yards outside each square.
  • 7 players: three squares (3 carriers + 3 defenders) plus 1 "counter" who counts and announces every successful escape out loud ("3 escapes!"), calls the cadence, and swaps into a square each round (~45-60 sec) so nobody waits long.
  • 6 players: exactly the three squares (3v3) — no counter, carriers call their own escapes.
  • 5 players: two squares plus the counter.
  • Skill sequence: (1) Monday recap — get low, wide base, body between ball and defender; (2) FEEL the defender's pressure as they push in from behind; (3) the moment they push hard, TURN AWAY — pivot the other way and dribble to the escape cone with the ball glued to your feet, not kicked ahead.
  • Progression: (1) shadow pressure — defender follows, no contact; (2) live light pressure; (3) full pressure — carrier must turn and reach the cone within 3 seconds or the defender wins the ball.
  • Coach cues: "feel the push — GO the other way", "low and wide", "ball glued to your feet", "turn, don't panic".
  • Watch for: carriers turning INTO the pressure instead of away, heads staying down after the turn, and the ball being kicked too far ahead — stop and reset, never punish.

5:55 – 6:10 PM
Escape to Space — Beat the Wolf
0/5
  • Equipment: 6-8 balls, ~10 cones, 2 pinnies for the defenders.
  • Objective: put the shield-turn-escape into a live keep-away where the ESCAPE ends in a PASS.
  • Setup: TWO keep-away grids (~12x8y each), 2 attackers vs 1 defender per grid; spare balls outside the grids.
  • Rule: attackers keep possession; when an attacker is under pressure they must SHIELD (hold 2-3 seconds) → TURN → PASS to the teammate in space = 1 point.
  • Defender wins the ball = quick restart, no penalty.
  • 7 players: both grids (2v1 + 2v1 = 6) plus the roaming "Wolf" (7th player) who pressures whichever carrier holds the ball too long — rewarding quick shield-turn-pass; the Wolf changes grids freely; rotate the Wolf and the defenders every round so all 7 get every role.
  • 6 players: exactly the two 2v1 grids, no Wolf.
  • 5 players: one 2v1 grid + one 1v1 shield-escape duel (reuse the Shield, Turn & Go concept).
  • Coach cues: "feel them, turn away, find your mate", "mate — give an angle", "shield 1, turn 2, pass 3".
  • Watch for: support movement off the ball (the teammate stepping to give a passing angle), pass quality (soft, to the space) after the escape, and carriers turning into pressure — praise the quick shield-turn-pass loudly.

6:10 – 6:30 PM
Shield & Share Scrimmage
0/5
  • Equipment: 1 ball, pinnies (2 colors), 4 cones or 2 small goals.
  • Setup: ~25x15y field with two small goals (4x2 or cone goals), two teams in pinnies.
  • Scoring: a normal goal = 1 point; a goal that BEGAN with a 3-second shield followed by one pass = 2 points ("shield, share, score").
  • This makes shield-escape-pass the scoring mechanic — the direct evolution of Monday's 5-second-shield bonus, and it gives every player a reason to protect first, then share.
  • 7 players: 3v3 + 1 "Joker" who plays for whichever team has the ball — the Joker floats, never defends, and sprints to the other team on turnover (mirrors the approved Star concept from the Week 3 plan); rotate the Joker every 3-4 min.
  • 6 players: straight 3v3.
  • 5 players: 2v2 + Joker.
  • Coach cues: "shield three seconds — share — score", "after you escape, look up — who's open?".
  • Watch for: after shielding, does the player look to pass to a teammate rather than dribbling into trouble; celebrate every shield → pass → goal sequence loudly.
  • e.g. which goals started with a shield + pass, did players look up after escaping or dribble into trouble...
