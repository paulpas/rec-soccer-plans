Arrival Warm-Up: “Look Both Ways”
0/4

Every player has a ball in a loose 20x20 area for easy dribbling. Coach walks behind players and holds up fingers; on a whistle the player looks over their shoulder, calls the number, then keeps dribbling. This builds the habit of checking behind you before the ball arrives.

5:40 – 5:48 PM
Welcome & “Look Both Ways”
0/4

Gather the team: “What do you do before you cross the street? You look both ways!” Today the ball is the person crossing, and the middle of the field is the street. Before the ball gets to you, look behind you to see where the danger (the defender) is, then go the other way.

5:48 – 6:02 PM
Drill 1: Crossing the Street
0/7

Setup: 20x50 field. Mark the middle section (about 10 yards) across the full width as the street — only defenders may be in it. One defender stands in the street. One line of players waits at the start end beside the coach. One receiver waits at the far end, one defender stands in the street.

The rep: The front player faces the coach, back to the street. Coach passes the ball. As it comes in, the player looks over both shoulders to find which side the defender is on, takes their touch the opposite way, then passes across the street to a player at the other end. That completes the rep.

Rotation (one line, everyone cycles): after passing, the passer goes to defense (into the street); the old defender goes to offense (the receiver at the far end); the old receiver goes to the back of the line. The line moves up and the next player steps in as passer. Everyone cycles line → passer → defense → offense → back to the line.

1. Crossing the Street — one rep

→ pass / touch● passer● receiver● defender● coach
Coach feeds the front player, who faces the coach with their back to the street. As the ball arrives they look over both shoulders, find where the defender is, take their touch to the opposite side and pass across the street to the receiver. Then everyone rotates: passer to defense, defender to offense (receiver), receiver to the back of the line.
6:02 – 6:14 PM
Drill 2: Make It Harder
0/5

Same field, same rep. Pick one progression at a time: (1) the defender starts in the middle of the street and only picks a side when the coach passes; (2) the coach passes a little firmer; (3) the receiver has to move to show for the pass. Keep each rep short so nobody waits long.

6:14 – 6:30 PM
Game: The Street Game
0/4

Small-sided game on the same field. Attackers must pass across the street rather than dribble through it; only defenders may enter it. Rotate who defends the street every minute or two. (This game is a suggestion to finish the session — swap it out if you prefer.)

2. Street Game (optional)

→ pass / touch● passer● receiver● defender● coach● other team
Same 20x50 field. Attackers may not dribble through the street — they must pass across it. Only defenders may stand in it. Rotate who defends in the street every minute or two.
