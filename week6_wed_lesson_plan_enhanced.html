Arrival Warm-Up & "Open Up" Footwork
0/5

Start with a quick-feet "Open Up" warm-up (no ball) so their first touch has a plan. Then free ball-mastery in a 20x15 yard space. The theme for the whole session: don’t stop the ball — touch it into space.

1. "Open Up" footwork — open the body to space

→ pass / first touch● passer● receiver● defender● attacker
Loose feet: quick small steps, then ONE big check step opening your shoulders to face the open space — ready to receive. Coach calls the direction (left/right/front); explode open to it.
5:40 – 5:48 PM
Welcome & The First-Touch Rally
0/4

Gather the team and name the problem plainly: “Today we fix the first touch. When the ball comes at us, do we stop it at our feet — which lets the defender take it — or do we send it to the other foot and pass back in one motion?” Demo: receive a pass, stop it (it’s gone), then receive and tap it to the other foot and pass back in one motion (we keep it). Ask them which one they’re going to practice.

5:48 – 6:00 PM
Drill 1: "Pass to a Foot, Pass Back" — Receive to the Other Foot
0/6

Pairs stand about 10 ft apart. The passer plays the ball straight at the receiver’s foot. The receiver gets it and, in ONE motion, taps it to their OTHER foot and passes it back — then it repeats, so the ball goes past everyone’s feet in a circle. Cue: pass to a foot, receive to the other foot, pass back — one motion, no stopping.

2. "Pass to a Foot, Pass Back" — receive to the other foot, pass back

→ pass / first touch● passer● receiver● defender● attacker
Pairs 10 ft apart, then link into one big circle. Pass the ball straight at a player’s foot. That player receives it and, in ONE motion, taps it to their OTHER foot and passes it back. Doesn’t matter which foot — as long as you receive and direct it to the other foot for the return pass. The ball circulates past everyone’s feet.
6:00 – 6:12 PM
Drill 2: "Receive, Turn & Go" — 1v1 Under Pressure
0/6

Add the context they’re missing: a defender pressing. Play the ball into the receiver just before the defender gets there. Their first touch MUST go PAST the defender into the open space and turn to attack the goal. A dead touch loses the ball — so the first touch has to be decisive and into space.

3. "Receive, Turn,amp; Go" — 1v1 against pressure

→ pass / first touch● passer● receiver● defender● attacker
Now there’s a defender. Play the ball into the receiver just before the defender gets there. Their first touch must go PAST the defender into the open space — turning to attack the goal. A dead touch here loses the ball, so it has to be decisive.
6:12 – 6:28 PM
Game: Winner Stays
0/4

2 small goals across a half-field. 1v1 (or 2v2) to a small goal: the attacker receives the pass heading toward the defender and MUST receive into space and turn to score. If they keep the ball / score they stay on; the loser passes to the next challenger. Winner-stays = constant pressure, so the first touch into space is the whole game.

4. Game: Winner Stays 1v1 / 2v2

→ pass / first touch● passer● receiver● defender● attacker
1v1 (or 2v2) to a small goal. Attacker receives the pass into the defender’s back — they MUST receive into space and turn to score. If they keep the ball / score they stay; the loser passes to the next challenger in line. Pressure makes the first touch matter.
6:28 – 6:40 PM
Cool-Down & Team Chat
0/3

Light rolling passes and easy touches. Team chat: which was harder — receiving across the body to the other foot, or the same foot? Where did you send your first touch when the defender was on you? What’s one thing to fix before next time?
