5:55 – 6:10 PM
Keep Baby Yoda Alive
0/6

Baby Yoda's first-ever game — keep it basic. Small open area: a 20x20 yard square or the field center circle — simpler than the long field; this is an intro. Three players (A, B, C) clip to Baby Yoda with ~6-foot leashes at their waist belts and make a triangle around him. A fourth player, D, clips to Player C with one more ~6-foot leash — D's simple job: step and feel how C reacts (no tactics). The rule: Yoda never touches the ground — if it does, Yoda 'dies' and everyone freezes, resets, no punishment. Five focus points: (1) know the physical constraint — Yoda dies if it touches the ground, and we reset with a smile; (2) keep roughly even spacing around Yoda; (3) move while holding that spacing; (4) see that if one player moves, the others may need to adjust; (5) begin to feel the group as ONE connected unit — not three separate players. This first intro is NOT defensive cover, pressure, balance, switching, rotations, passing combos, or scanning — those are saved for later practices. Start standing still and feel the tension: each player steps out until their leash is taut but Yoda stays aloft. Then move together on coach calls — 'step left together', 'turn right together', small shuffling steps, leashes stay taut. One teaching point: one player's movement can influence another player's positioning. 7 players: A, B, C on leashes + D connected to C + 3 on a ball-mastery station (dribble through cones, toe taps, keepy-uppies), swap groups every ~4-5 min; 6 players: A, B, C, D + 2 on ball-mastery — the connected 4th concept stays the same.

6:10 – 6:30 PM
Two Yodas, One Star
0/5

Two goals, one at each end of the 40x10 yard field — a Baby Yoda sits in each goal between two cones (only one plushie exists; the second goal uses a stand-in, a second plushie or a cone/marker called "Baby Yoda's friend"). Split into two small teams (7=3v3, 6=3v2, 5=2v2) plus one global Star. The Star plays for whichever team has the ball — the guaranteed open outlet — and never defends; on a turnover the Star sprints to join the new attacking team. Score by touching the ball to the enemy Yoda. Yoda is in play for everyone: no fouls for touching Yoda, defenders may stand in front of their own Yoda to block. Rotate who is the Star every 3-4 minutes so everyone gets a turn; rebalance teams between rounds. Spacing lesson: when we keep space, Baby Yoda stays alive; when we bunch up, the Star is wide open, the other team finds them, and your Yoda gets touched.
