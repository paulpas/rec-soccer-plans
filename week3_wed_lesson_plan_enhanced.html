Baby Yoda Cone Collection
0/5

40 yard long x 10 yard wide field. Scatter multiple cones in 4 colors at random spots — e.g., 2 cones of each color (8 total) — with far ones hard to reach. A set of 3 players wears belts with 6-yard leashes attached to the Baby Yoda plushie in the center. Baby Yoda must stay aloft: the set keeps leashes taut while EACH player collects every cone of their assigned color. Coach assigns 3 of the 4 colors each attempt (the 4th color is a decoy) — all 3 players must finish within 1 minute for the attempt to count. If Baby Yoda touches the ground (a leash goes slack), 'he dies' and the attempt resets — try again. Rotate sets of 3 so everyone gets turns; bench players do ball-mastery nearby (dribble through cones, keepy-uppies) and rotate in when called.

6:10 – 6:30 PM
Spacing Game Scenarios
0/5

Walk through specific situations from the game we played. Set up small-sided games based on roster: 7 players = 4v3, 6 players = 3v3, 5 players = 3v2. One player (designated "tension keeper") stands in the center holding the Baby Yoda leashes — they sit out of the game while the rest play. Rotate the tension keeper every 5 minutes so no one kid holds it the whole time. When you coach calls "Drop!" or notices the team bunching up, the tension keeper lets Baby Yoda fall — direct cause and effect. When Baby Yoda hits the ground, everyone freezes for 3 seconds, then the coach says "Go" and the scenario continues. Goal: they see that keeping space prevents the collapse. No passing requirement — focus on movement, positioning, and spatial awareness. After each scenario, pause and ask: 'What did you feel when Baby Yoda fell? What will you try next time?'
