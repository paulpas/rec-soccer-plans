Yoda's Deep Breath
0/5

Last week we built Baby Yoda's first rule: A, B, C hold the triangle on ~6-ft leashes, D connected to C — ONE CONNECTED UNIT. This week the unit learns to BREATHE: change size and stay alive. Coach calls "Spread wide!" — all four step OUT until the leashes are taut and the triangle is big, Yoda stays centered. "Squeeze in!" — bring the shape smaller toward the middle, NO slack, NO dropping Yoda, no bumping him. "Freeze!" — hold the shape exactly where it is. Start slow, then mix the calls faster. If a leash goes slack or Yoda touches the ground: "Yoda's asleep!" — everyone freezes, reset with a smile, restart at medium size. D's job stays simple: step and feel how C adjusts as the unit expands and contracts. 7 players: A, B, C on leashes + D connected to C + 3 on the ball-mastery station (dribble-cone figure-8s, toe taps, keepy-uppies). 6 players: A, B, C, D + 2 on the station. 5 players: A, B, C, D + 1 on the station. Swap groups every ~4-5 minutes so every kid gets leash time.

5:55 – 6:10 PM
Pick Your Gate
0/5

Back to the ring — 20x20 square, 4 pressure players around 1 carrier. Last week the carrier got out through ANY gate with a shield: low and wide, body between ball and defense, ball comes WITH you. This week the gates are guarded — the carrier must PICK the gate the guardian is NOT guarding. Guardians guard their gate — or gamble by stepping out to pressure, which OPENS their gate behind them. Carrier: scan both gates, pick the open one (or the weaker side), commit, explode through body-first with the ball. 7 players: 4 ring + 1 carrier + 2 guardians (one per gate). 6 players: 4 ring + 1 carrier + 1 guardian who must choose ONE gate — the carrier reads and takes the open one. 5 players: 4 ring + 1 carrier — gates unguarded, a calibration round back to Week 3. Attempts ~20-30 seconds, coach counts "1, 2, 3 — GO!" Steal or failed exit = quick reset, no penalty. Rotate carrier every attempt; guardians rotate too — everyone gets all three jobs.

NEW TWIST: a goal scored while your team is spread out wide counts DOUBLE — both wide attackers are outside the width markers when the pass or shot happens. Rotate who is the Star every 3-4 minutes so everyone gets a turn; rebalance teams between rounds.
