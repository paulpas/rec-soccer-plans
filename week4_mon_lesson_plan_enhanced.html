Space Scrimmage — Lava Zones
0/8
  • Equipment: 1 ball, ~12 cones (1 large diamond + 3 smaller diamonds), 2 colors of pinnies.
  • Objective: force field awareness — players must look around, spread out, and become available. Eliminate bunching.
  • Setup: two halves. Left half: one large diamond in the center (takes up the whole middle of the half). Right half: 3 smaller diamonds. All diamonds are "lava" — the ball carrier cannot enter.
  • Rules: ball carrier cannot enter any lava zone (no exceptions). Players must look around for open space and stay available for passes.
  • 7 players: 3v3 + 1 floating player who plays for whoever has the ball.
  • 6 players: 3v3.
  • 5 players: 2v2 + 1 floating.
  • Rotate teams every 5-7 minutes.
  • Coach cues: "look around!", "where's the open space?", "don't bunch up!", "lava burns — stay out!"
  • Watch for: players bunching, not looking around, ignoring open space — stop and reset, never punish.
