Green Light, Red Light with Footwork
0/6

20x15 yard grid. Each player has a ball. Coach calls lights. Green = fast, Yellow = slow, Red = stop with sole and place a body part on the ball (call: right foot, left foot, inside, heel, both feet, outside). Body parts called unpredictably to force quick footwork stops.

5:40 – 5:55 PM
Backside Protection + Spin Move
0/6

10x10 yard square. Paired up (1 attacker with ball, 1 defender without). Defender pushes into attacker's back. Attacker must: (1) show their back — turn away with feet wider than shoulder-width, (2) pivot on ONE foot and spin 180 degrees, (3) push off and drive away in the new direction. Rotate roles every 30 seconds. After 10 min, do a final round where attacker starts with back already to defender (no wind-up).

5:55 – 6:07 PM
Pirates
0/4

15x15 yard square. Split into two teams: Pirates (2 players, no balls) and Sailors (3 players, each with a ball). Round 1: Pirates kick Sailors' balls out with feet. Round 2: Pirates can also reach in with hands to PULL the ball from Sailors' feet. Switch roles after each round.

6:07 – 6:15 PM
King of the Ring
0/3

20x20 yard square. All 5 players inside with a ball. Protect YOUR ball, kick OTHER players' balls out. If your ball goes out, you're out — stand outside. Last person standing with their ball wins. 30-second tiebreaker if two are left.
