Red Light, Green Light with Ball
0/4

20x15 yard grid. Each player has a ball. Green = dribble fast, Yellow = dribble slow. On Red: stop the ball with your sole, or OPT to do toe taps while moving the ball forward (their choice). Mix it up and keep calls unpredictable.

5:55 – 6:10 PM
Pirates
0/4

15x15 yard square. 2 Pirates (no ball) vs 3 Sailors (each with a ball). Round 1 — normal style: Pirates kick Sailors' balls out of the square. Round 2 — escalate: Pirates must reach in and PULL the ball away with their hands. Switch roles.

6:10 – 6:20 PM
Juggling
0/3

Open space, each player has a ball. Demonstrate: ankle relaxed, soft touch, ball pops up just above the foot. Count consecutive touches. Goal: beat your own personal best, not each other's.

6:20 – 6:30 PM
King of the Ring
0/4

20x20 yard square. All players inside with a ball. Protect YOUR ball, kick others' balls out. If your ball goes out, you're out. Last one standing wins. 30-sec tiebreaker if two remain. Shielding/backside protection is taught ad-hoc here — show kids how to put their body between defender and ball on the fly.

6:30 – 6:45 PM
Target Practice
0/5

2 goals (5 ft wide), spaced 10 ft apart, facing the same direction. Cones: one beside each goal post (3 points each) and one in the center of each goal (1 point). Two lines, one per goal. Dribble 10 ft to the marker, then make the pass — you must be MOVING when you pass; the movement gives you the space to strike cleanly. Everyone goes 3 times.
