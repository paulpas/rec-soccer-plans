Arrival Warm-Up: Scissors & Feints
0/5

One player per 2x2 yard square, cone in the middle. First no ball: scissors footwork (open-close, open-close) around the cone, and body feints — drop a shoulder or turn the hips, then go the other way. Then add the ball and repeat everything with the ball at your feet.

Keep it slow. The point is to sell the move, not to be quick. Praise the feint that makes you flinch.

Welcome & “Sell the Move”
0/4

Gather the team and name the idea: “A feint only works if it makes the other person move. Sell it — do the move, then go the other way.”

Today’s rule for every drill: one footwork move to go around a person. Slow motion is good — a slow feint that works beats a fast one that does not.

5:48 – 6:05 PM
Drill 1: 1v1 Feint & Go
0/7

Setup: Offense line at one end, defense line at the other end. One attacker steps off the offense line and starts dribbling in; the first defender steps off the defense line and approaches them.

The defender is presence only — they are in the way but do not try to take the ball (no active defense today in this drill).

The rep: the attacker does ONE footwork move (scissors, body feint, toe-flick...) to go around the defender, then dribbles to the far goal and shoots.

Reset: when everyone gets to the other side, reset — offense and defense swap ends.

Drill 2: Rondo 6v2, then 5+1v2
0/6

Setup: 8 players: 6 outside in the ring, 2 in the middle defending.

After 5 minutes: one of the 6 outside players moves into the middle as the support — a passing target only, never defends. Now it is 5+1 vs 2.

Rotation: the defense CAN actively steal the ball. When a defender wins it, that defender rotates OUT and the outside player who lost the ball rotates IN to defend.

Game: Lava Scrimmage 4v4
0/5

Setup: 4v4 (8 players), one goal at each end. The middle of the field is a lava zone — players cannot enter it, but the ball can.

Scoring rule: a goal only counts if the ball was passed 3+ times before it went in. A goal after 2 passes or fewer does not count. This forces teams to build through passes instead of running straight at the goal.

Rotate teams every few minutes so everyone plays both sides.
