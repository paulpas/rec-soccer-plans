Back-to-Back Power Shielding
0/5

5x5 yard squares. Two players back-to-back in each square. PHASE 1 (3 min): Bend knees, get low, back flat against each other. On 'GO' push with shoulders — try to move the other person out. No hands, no arm shoving. First pushed out loses. PHASE 2 (3 min): Same setup, but now lean and ROLL around them so you end up in front. Step around their side, turn your shoulders. PHASE 3 (4 min): Add a ball to each pair's feet. Push/roll → whoever gets in front takes the ball and dribbles away. Coach cue: 'Get low like a wrestler, push with your whole body, roll around not just push!'

5:55 – 6:10 PM
Pirates with Shielding Twist
0/4

15x15 yard square. 2 Pirates vs 3 Sailors. Normal Pirates rules, but add the Shielding Twist: after a Sailor loses their ball, they get to go into 'Shield Mode' for 3 seconds — back to the Pirate, knees bent, wide stance, arm up slightly. If the Pirate can't knock it out in those 3 seconds, the Sailor gets the ball back. Teaches Pirates to finish quick, teaches Sailors to hold ground.

6:10 – 6:30 PM
Shielding Game
0/5

2 small goals (4x2 ft) spaced 15 ft apart on each end of a 25x15 yard field. Split into two teams (3 vs 2, one sub rotates every 3 min). Play with the stronger team on defense, then switch sides halfway through. Add a bonus point: if a player shields the ball for 5 full seconds without losing it, their team gets a bonus point (worth 2 goals). This makes shielding a scoring mechanic, not just a defensive skill. Rotate teams frequently so everyone plays.
