Phase 1 — V-Push: Three Steps

● ball start→ sole pullback▼ outside push
Pull back with sole, push out the side. This creates a V-shape with the ball — the "V-push" that's foundational for every first touch and change of direction.

Phase 1 — Gates: Playing Through Cones

● player with ball⋮ gate (two cones)
Every player has a ball. Dribble through gates using inside-outside touches. After passing through, find a new gate as a pair.

Top-down triangle with pass-and-move arrows. Magic man in center always available.

Phase 2 — Triangle Carousel: 3v0 + Magic Man

● passer (must run to passed-to cone)○ magic man neutral
Three players rotate after every pass. The magic man is always available for a lay-off. Pass and move are ONE action — the run starts as the ball leaves your foot.

Same triangle with one passive defender in center — players use V-touch to create space.

Phase 3 — Game Application: Triangle + Passive Defender

● attacker with ball✕ passive defender (cone + bib)
The passive defender creates a real decision point. V-touch changes direction to escape pressure — this is exactly what happens when a defender closes you in a match.
