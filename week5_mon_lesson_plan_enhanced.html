Half-Field Lava Scrimmage
0/8
  • Equipment: 1 ball, ~12 cones (large center diamond + sideline goals), 2 colors of pinnies.
  • Objective: field awareness, spacing, and decision-making under pressure — the lava zone forces players to look around and spread out.
  • Setup: half-field with a large diamond in the center (the "lava zone"). Goals on each sideline (not end zones).
  • Rules: the ball can go through the lava, but no players can — except one special floating player on offense who NEVER leaves the lava.
  • 7 players: 3v3 + 1 floating player on offense (never leaves the lava, plays for whoever has the ball). One player sits out each round.
  • Wingers can only go as far as the middle of the field and cannot cross to the other side — a left winger stays on the left, a right winger stays on the right.
  • Rotate teams and the floating player every 5-7 minutes so everyone gets a turn in the lava.
  • Coach cues: "look around!", "where's the open space?", "don't bunch up!", "lava burns — stay out!", "wingers stay on your side!"
  • Watch for: players bunching, not looking around, wingers crossing over — stop and reset, never punish.
