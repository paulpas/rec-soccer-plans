Pass Into Space — Runner Receives Ahead

• passer• runner△ space
Player A passes to the space ahead of Player B — not at their feet. Player B runs onto the ball. If a cone "defender" is blocking the direct lane, use footwork to move the ball until a passing lane opens, then play the ball into space.

Seated Juggling — Toe Angle Drill

● ball path↑ ball direction• foot contact
Sit on the ground with legs extended. Hold the ball at chest height, drop it, and tap it up with the toes pointed DOWN — hit the ball with the laces area of your foot. Toes pointed down = ball goes up. Toes pointed up = ball goes forward away from you.
BAREFOOT CHALLENGE: Have players try this drill barefoot. If they focus on proper form — toes pointed down, soft controlled touches — they won't hurt their foot. If it stings, their toes are pointing up. Fix the form, not the footwear.
